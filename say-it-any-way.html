Say It Any Way

CustomersHarder, but possible
  1. 1

    The installer and the homeowner do not share a language.

  2. 2

    Each writes in their own language and the app translates over the same photo.

  3. 3

    The homeowner presses the control once while I step back.

  4. 4

    The homeowner and I tick Done, so she knows how it works.

Problem

The customer and I do not share a language, and I leave her unsure how her new thing works.

What you gain

She uses her new device on day one, and I skip the call back.

Brings the next customer

A customer who understands her new device trusts me with the next one.

Why build it

Customers who cannot use what was installed feel lost, and this fixes it before the pro leaves.

Who has it

Providers with Spanish-speaking customers, installers of thermostats and locks, older homeowners.
